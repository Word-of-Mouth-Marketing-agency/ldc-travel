# Destination research sources

Research/review date: 2026-09-21

The public destination copy is original, concise paraphrase. These sources support the stable cultural, geographic, and experience themes used in the preview/demo content. Entry rules, visas, safety, and other changeable requirements are intentionally not presented as permanent public facts.

## Turkey

- [GoTürkiye](https://goturkiye.com/) — national tourism guide; refreshed the Istanbul, Bosphorus, Aegean/Mediterranean coast, cultural-route, and destination-diversity themes.
- [Cappadocia GoTürkiye](https://cappadocia.goturkiye.com/) — official destination guide; refreshed the fairy-chimney landscape, valleys, cave spaces, food, and outdoor-experience themes.

## Russia

- [Kremlin and Red Square, Moscow — UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/545) — supported the historic-centre, Kremlin, Red Square, and architectural-heritage references.
- [Historic Centre of Saint Petersburg — UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/540) — supported the canals, embankments, architectural ensembles, museums, and cultural-city framing.
- [Visit Petersburg](https://visit-petersburg.ru/en/) — official city tourism portal; supported the city-guide and cultural-visitor framing. Changeable entry, transport, and safety information is intentionally excluded from evergreen copy.

## Bali

- [Bali — Indonesia Travel](https://www.indonesia.travel/us/en/explore-indonesia/bali-nusa-tenggara/bali/) — official Ministry of Tourism destination guide; refreshed the distinct Bali framing around Ubud, beaches, rice landscapes, temples, cultural rituals, and nature.

## Georgia

- [Georgia Travel](https://georgia.travel/) — official Georgian tourism portal; refreshed Tbilisi, Caucasus nature, regional variety, food, wine, and cultural-monument themes.
- [Visit Kazbegi — Georgia Travel](https://georgia.travel/cities-towns/stepantsminda) — supported the Stepantsminda/Kazbegi mountain and Gergeti context.
- [Kakheti — Georgia Travel](https://georgia.travel/regions/kakheti) — supported Kakheti's regional food, wine, landscape, and heritage framing.

## Indonesia

- [Yogyakarta — Indonesia Travel](https://www.indonesia.travel/gb/en/destination/java/yogyakarta) — official Ministry of Tourism destination guide; refreshed the broader Indonesia angle around Java, Yogyakarta, palace/craft culture, heritage, and volcanic/nature themes.
- [Lombok — Indonesia Travel](https://www.indonesia.travel/gb/en/destination/bali-nusa-tenggara/west-nusa-tenggara/lombok) — supported the broader-archipelago framing, Lombok coastline, mountain, island, and Sasak-culture references.

## Thailand

- [Tourism Authority of Thailand](https://www.tourismthailand.org/) — official tourism authority portal; refreshed the Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Phuket/island, food, culture, nature, and regional-seasonality framing.
- [TAT language and culture guidance](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Articles/plan-your-trip-language-and-culture) — supported the cautious language note about English availability in Bangkok and major visitor areas.
