# Destination research sources

Research date: 2026-09-21

The public destination copy is original, concise paraphrase. These sources support the stable cultural, geographic, and experience themes used in the preview/demo content. Entry rules, visas, safety, and other changeable requirements are intentionally not presented as permanent public facts.

## Turkey

- [GoTürkiye](https://goturkiye.com/) — national tourism guide; supported the Istanbul, Antalya/coast, cultural-route, and destination diversity themes.
- [Cappadocia GoTürkiye](https://cappadocia.goturkiye.com/) — official destination guide; supported the fairy-chimney landscape, valleys, cave spaces, food, and outdoor-experience themes.

## Russia

- [Kremlin and Red Square, Moscow — UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/545) — supported the historic-centre, Kremlin, Red Square, and architectural-heritage references.
- [Historic Centre of Saint Petersburg — UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/540) — supported the canals, embankments, architectural ensembles, museums, and cultural-city framing.
- [Visit Petersburg](https://visit-petersburg.ru/en/) — official city tourism portal; supported the city-guide, attractions, and visitor-information framing.

## Bali

- [Bali — Indonesia Travel](https://www.indonesia.travel/gb/en/destination/bali-nusa-tenggara/bali) — official Ministry of Tourism destination guide; supported Ubud, beaches, rice terraces, temples, cultural rituals, and nature themes.

## Georgia

- [Georgia Travel](https://georgia.travel/) — official Georgian tourism portal; supported Tbilisi, Caucasus nature, regional variety, food, wine, and cultural-monument themes.
- [Destinations in Georgia](https://georgia.travel/destinations-in-georgia) — supported the contrast between mountain landscapes, Black Sea areas, historic architecture, and modern city sights.
- [Places Georgia Travel](https://places.georgia.travel/en) — supported the food, wine, culture, and discovery-oriented experience framing.

## Indonesia

- [Yogyakarta — Indonesia Travel](https://www.indonesia.travel/gb/en/destination/java/yogyakarta/yogyakarta) — official Ministry of Tourism destination guide; supported Java, Yogyakarta, palace/craft culture, Borobudur and Prambanan context, and volcanic/nature themes.
- [Lombok — Indonesia Travel](https://www.indonesia.travel/id/id/destination/bali-nusa-tenggara/west-nusa-tenggara/lombok) — supported the broader-archipelago framing, Lombok coastline, mountain, island, and Sasak-culture references.

## Thailand

- [Tourism Authority of Thailand](https://thai.tourismthailand.org/) — official tourism authority portal; supported Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Phuket/island, food, culture, nature, and regional-seasonality framing.
- [TAT Taste of Happiness — official route brochure](https://tourismproduct.tourismthailand.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Brochure-TAT-Taste-of-Happiness-5-Routes-ENG.pdf) — supported the Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Phuket, and multi-region route themes.
